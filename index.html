Attunement settings ·

Overrides are for testing. Primaries must be attuned and cannot exceed the attunement limit.

Daily preparations allow 2 attunements at levels 1–6, 3 at 7–14 and 4 at 15–20. Medium gains a second primary at level 9 through Dual Invocation. With no level set, the planner allows up to 4 attunements for reference.

Choose a primary from your attuned apparitions at daily preparations, on Refocus, or with an ability such as Circle of Spirits. If your primary is dispersed, select a replacement from those still attuned.
